Scout · relevance grading · September 2026

Which model decides if a post is worth a reply, and where can it run?

Fifteen open models and three hosted frontier models replayed against the same frozen snapshots and human labels, on two prompts, with four of them re-run quantised on a local box. The short version: the prompt moved the numbers far more than the model, and a 26B mixture-of-experts at 4-bit on frink matches the hosted baseline at under three seconds a case.

Figure 2 · recorded prompt → topical prompt

The prompt, not the model

The recorded production prompt carries a reject list (announcements, news, commentary). Models that obey it lose twenty cases to false negatives; gemini ignores it. A subject-matter-only rubric recovers them for every open model.

Figure 3 · topical prompt, error split

Where the remaining errors are

Under the topical prompt the misses are mostly false positives, and they are the same posts for nearly every model: on-topic announcements, promos, paper headlines and tutorial plugs that the labels mark not relevant. That is a rubric question, not a model ranking.

How much is noise

Every cell on this page is one draw. Only one configuration was drawn more than once without failed cases; this is its spread. llama-4-scout was also drawn twice on both projects (34 then 33 on agent-ops, 20 then 20 on agent-evals) but with failed cases in one draw each.
